STONE: Soft-Label Tone Classification for Taiwanese Hokkien

via CTC-Guided Contrastive Prototypes on Self-Supervised Models

Yu-Kai Guo · Chen-Wei Hsu · Hung-yi Lee

National Taiwan University

Paper (PDF) Back to Taigi Research →

01Overview

STONE labels the tone of every syllable in Taiwanese Hokkien speech. A self-supervised speech model with CTC alignment locates each syllable, and the syllable is compared with eight learned tone prototypes; the result is a ranked list of tones with confidence scores rather than a single hard label.

The correct tone depends on where a syllable sits. Tone sandhi in Taiwanese Hokkien is positional: inside a word a syllable always takes its sandhi tone; before punctuation it keeps its citation tone; between words it depends on whether the speaker pauses. The same syllable can therefore have different correct tones in different positions, and the examples below show how the classifier handles each position.

中文摘要

STONE 是臺語（台灣閩南語）的聲調分類器：以自監督語音模型和 CTC 對齊找出每個音節，再和八個聲調原型比較，輸出帶信心分數的聲調排名。臺語的連讀變調依位置而定：詞內一定變調，標點前維持本調，詞與詞之間則要看有沒有停頓。本頁以 SuíSiann 真人錄音展示模型在不同變調位置上的判斷，並附上各位置的可靠度統計。

03How reliable is this?

The five recordings above were selected, not sampled at random: each has a strict alignment (one CTC segment per syllable), Score_tone ≥ 0.85, Coverage ≥ 0.85, at least four informative sandhi categories, and every key syllable confident and within its lawful tones.

Across 300 SuíSiann sentences, word-internal sandhi is reliable: among confident syllables, the top prediction is lawful 83–100% of the time in every word-internal category. The neutral-tone syllable itself is not (25%), and neither are tones 4, 5 and 8 right before a neutral tone (14–20%).

04How the score is computed

For each syllable, let d1 ≤ d2 ≤ … ≤ d8 be the distances from its embedding to the eight tone prototypes, sorted, and let tone i be the tone at rank i. Then

mi = (di+1 − di) / (di+1 + di)

scorei = mi / (di + mi)

The score is high when the syllable is close to a prototype and clearly closer to it than to the next one. “Predicted” shows the rank-1 tone and its score; a syllable counts as confident when that score is at least 0.7. Scores of different ranks are computed separately and do not sum to 1.
