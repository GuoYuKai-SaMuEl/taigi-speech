Fantastic Taiwanese Hokkien Corpora and Where to Find Them

Bootstrapping Low-Resource Speech and Text via Off-the-Shelf LLMs and TTS

Yu-Kai Guo · Chen-Wei Hsu · Hung-Wei Chen · Hung-Yi Lee

National Taiwan University

Paper (PDF) Back to Taigi Research →

01Overview

This page accompanies the speech-synthesis experiment of the paper. We synthesize Taiwanese Hokkien with Gemini 3.1 Flash TTS, a general-purpose text-to-speech model without official support for the language, and compare three input conditions: Han-ji (Chinese characters) only, Tâi-lô (romanization) only, and both. Ten native speakers rated every clip blind on pronunciation, naturalness, and accent on a 1–5 scale.

Tâi-lô turns out to be essential for correct pronunciation: with Han-ji alone the pronunciation score drops to 2.82, compared with 3.75–3.76 whenever Tâi-lô is given.

中文摘要

臺灣閩南語（臺語）是低資源語言，公開語料稀少且常受授權限制。本研究提出以現成的通用大型語言模型（LLM）與語音合成（TTS）模型，合成臺語文字與語音語料。在華語與臺語的雙向翻譯任務中，表現最佳的 Gemini 3.1 Pro Preview 已接近辭典參考譯文的品質，經微調的本地小型模型也能與許多大型雲端模型相當。

本頁展示論文中的語音合成實驗：以 Gemini 3.1 Flash TTS 分別輸入漢字、臺羅（Tâi-lô）及漢字加臺羅合成臺語語音，並由 10 位臺語母語者盲聽評分。結果顯示臺羅對正確發音不可或缺：只輸入漢字時，發音分數降至 2.82；有臺羅時則為 3.75–3.76（滿分 5 分）。

Key finding · Pronunciation, 1–5

Han-ji only 2.82
Han-ji + Tâi-lô 3.76
